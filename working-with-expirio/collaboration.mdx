We build your website in nine sequential steps. At every step, you know in advance:

- what we'll be doing,
- what we'll need from you,
- how long the work usually takes,
- what deliverable you'll receive,
- who approves it and how.

We don't start the next step until the previous one is closed. That way we're not making important decisions retroactively, and development never begins on top of an unapproved design.

If your internal process calls for different people to be involved or more time to approve things, we'll agree on that during the discovery call.

<Info>
  **Typical timeline: 2 to 4 months** from discovery call to launch.
</Info>

As a rough breakdown:

- strategy, structure, and content: 2 to 3 weeks,
- visual design: 3 to 5 weeks,
- development and internal testing: 4 to 8 weeks,
- client review and launch: 1 to 2 weeks.

Some of this work can run in parallel. A smaller site with content ready and fast approvals can come close to two months. A larger site or longer approval cycles push the project nearer to four.

Development is usually the longest part of the project. For a typical website, plan on roughly two months.

## What we need from you

The process doesn't require your involvement every day. But at predetermined points we need materials, feedback, or a decision. Until those are closed, we can't safely move to the next phase.

### One person responsible for decisions

You can involve as many colleagues as you like. But we need one person who, on behalf of your team:

- collects the feedback,
- resolves any conflicts within it,
- passes it to us consolidated,
- confirms the final decisions.

This is what keeps us from receiving contradictory requests, or being unsure which version of the feedback applies.

### Content by the agreed date

For the design we'll need real copy, photographs, information about your services or products, references, and other relevant materials.

If some of the content is still being produced, we'll agree together who prepares it and by when. Missing content can affect both the page designs and the launch date.

### Consolidated feedback

We need your feedback all at once, in the agreed place. Before you send it, it should be discussed internally within your team.

We set a deadline in advance for each round of feedback. If approval slips, we'll tell you what that means for the work ahead and for the launch date.

### The access we need

Before launch, we may need access to:

- your domain registrar,
- your current hosting,
- analytics tools,
- cookie management tools,
- services connected to your forms,
- any other systems integrated with the site.

We'll send you the list well in advance, including which access we need and what it will be used for.

## What happens after launch

After launch we check the site on the production domain and confirm to you that everything is working as planned.

From there, you can continue with regular management and development of the site. That might include:

- technical maintenance,
- editing and adding content,
- building new pages,
- analyzing traffic,
- conversion optimization,
- further development as your business needs it.

We define the scope of any ongoing collaboration separately, based on the support you need.
